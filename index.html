Privacy Policy — Kort Begrip

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Kort Begrip ("the app") is a flash card application for Android.

No data collection

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) or personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Any content you create (e.g. flash cards) stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Permissions

Any device permissions the app requests are used solely for its core functionality and not for data collection.

Third parties

The app does not integrate with third-party services that collect user data.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
